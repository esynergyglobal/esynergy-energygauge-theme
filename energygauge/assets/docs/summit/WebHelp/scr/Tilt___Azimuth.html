Tilt and Azimuth Angle

 

Tilt Angle Selection:

There are 3 ways to enter the value.

i) Click on the minus or plus button to select the value and then click on OK

ii) Click on the Face Down or Vertical or Face Up or deg Down-slope or 45 deg Up-Slope text to enter the selected value and then click OK.

iii) Click on the wheel to select the desired value and then click OK.

 

Azimuth Angle Selection:

There are 3 ways to enter the value.

i) Click on the minus or plus button to select the value and then click on OK

ii) Click on the E or W or N or S or NE or NW or SW or SE to enter the selected value and then click OK.

iii) Click on the wheel to select the desired value and then click OK.
