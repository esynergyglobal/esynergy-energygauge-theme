EnergyGauge Summit
 

EnergyGauge Summit is the Energy Code compliance tool for commercial buildings in Florida and other states in the USA. It was developed by the Florida Solar Energy Center. EnergyGauge Summit is a Windows-based product and will operate under most Microsoft Windows® versions.

The Florida Code version of the software has the following Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation compliance versions:

  • 2023 Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation (8th Edition)
  • 2020 Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation (7th Edition) and its 2022 suppliment
  • 2017 Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation (6th Edition)
  • 2014 Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation (5th Edition)
  • 2010 Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation
  • 2008 Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation

In  addition, serveral versions of ASHRAE and IECC national code compliance, ASHRAE Appendix G rating, LEED, and Commercial Building Tax-credit calculation are also available in the Premier version of the software
